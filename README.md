## 关于我

你好！我叫**谷昱宁，字盈秋**，也可以叫我**盈秋**。

目前就读于法国**巴黎萨克雷大学（Université Paris-Saclay）药学院**，攻读 **Development of Drugs and Health Products（D2HP）** 硕士学位。

我主要关注药物制剂技术、药物递送、天然产物及转化医学研究。科研之外，我也喜欢开发开源软件，尤其是 AI 辅助工作流、学习工具、桌面应用和解决日常需求的实用程序。

我的兴趣不局限于单一学科：一方面希望通过实验、分析与数据方法理解药物和健康问题；另一方面也希望运用软件工程与自动化，让复杂的工作变得更简单、可复现、可使用。

## 开源项目

我的项目大多源于实际需求：**减少重复操作、改善交互体验，并尽可能让用户掌控自己的工具和数据**。

### AI 与学习工具

**[DevRelay](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/DevRelay)**

本地运行、可审计的多模型软件开发编排工具。尝试衔接“规划 → 实现 → 审阅”的开发流程，减少在不同 AI 编程工具之间反复复制粘贴的工作，同时保留人工监督与最终决策。

**[课堂同传助手（LiveCaptions Translator）](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/LiveCaptions-Translator)**

针对外语课堂设计的实时转录与翻译工具，支持中文翻译、分节总结、课件辅助笔记和语音播报。该仓库是基于 [SakiRinn 原项目](https://github.com/SakiRinn/LiveCaptions-Translator) 开发的课堂场景特化分支，并非原项目官方版本。

### 应用与生产力

**[Evolune](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Evolune)** · Android / Wear OS

面向 Android 与 Wear OS 的本地优先应用项目，配有独立部署的 [Evolune 网站](https://evolune.yingqiu.me)。

**[纯粹番茄钟（Pure Pomodoro）](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/pure-pomodoro)** · Windows

轻量、完全离线的 Windows 番茄钟。支持从当前壁纸提取主题配色、专注与休息时段管理、快捷键等功能。

**[MP14Tools](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/MP14tools)** · Windows

为小米 Book Pro 14（2026）开发的设备优化工具，涵盖触摸板重按及 OEM 热键映射、刷新率与电源配置切换、后台进程效率管理等功能。

### 生活实用工具

**[Petit Coin · 方便点](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Petit-Coin)** · Flutter

法国公共厕所查询应用，提供地图、多语言界面，以及开放状态、无障碍设施等条件筛选，使用 OpenStreetMap 和城市开放数据。

**[Holland2Stay Notifier](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Holland2StayNotifier)** · Python / SQLite / Telegram

监测 Holland2Stay 可预订房源，并通过 Telegram 向订阅群组发送新房源通知。

[查看全部公开仓库 →](https://github.com/YingQiu0871?tab=repositories)

## About me

Hello! I'm **Yuning Gu**. You can also call me **Yingqiu**.

I'm currently pursuing an MSc in **Development of Drugs and Health Products (D2HP)** at the **Faculty of Pharmacy, Université Paris-Saclay**, France.

My academic interests include pharmaceutical formulation, drug delivery, natural products, and translational health research. Beyond the laboratory, I enjoy creating open-source software—from AI-assisted development workflows and learning tools to desktop applications and everyday utilities.

I am particularly interested in the connections between **experimental science, data analysis, software engineering, and practical product development**. Whether working on a formulation project or a software tool, I value clarity, reproducibility, and real-world usefulness.

## Open-source projects

Many of my projects begin with a practical need: **reducing repetitive work, improving usability, and keeping users in control of their tools and data**.

### AI and learning

- **[DevRelay](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/DevRelay)** — A local, auditable coordination layer for multi-model software development. It connects planning, implementation, and review workflows while preserving human oversight.
- **[LiveCaptions Translator — Classroom Edition](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/LiveCaptions-Translator)** — A classroom-oriented fork of [SakiRinn/LiveCaptions-Translator](https://github.com/SakiRinn/LiveCaptions-Translator), adapted for foreign-language lectures with real-time transcription, translation, topic summaries, slide-aware notes, and speech playback. Not an official upstream release.

### Apps and productivity

- **[Evolune](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Evolune)** — A local-first Android and Wear OS application, with a separately deployed [website](https://evolune.yingqiu.me).
- **[Pure Pomodoro](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/pure-pomodoro)** — A lightweight, offline Windows Pomodoro timer with wallpaper-inspired colors and focus controls.
- **[MP14Tools](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/MP14tools)** — A Windows hardware and power-management utility for Xiaomi Book Pro 14 (2026), including touchpad / OEM-key remapping and power profiles.

### Everyday utilities

- **[Holland2Stay Notifier](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Holland2StayNotifier)** — A Python, SQLite, and Telegram tool that notifies subscribers of newly available housing listings.
