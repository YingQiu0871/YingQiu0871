## Hello! · 你好！

My name is **Yuning Gu (谷昱宁)**. My **courtesy name (字)** is **Yingqiu (盈秋)**, and you're welcome to call me **Yingqiu**. I am an MSc student in **Development of Drugs and Health Products (D2HP)** at the Faculty of Pharmacy, **Université Paris-Saclay**, France.

My academic interests span pharmaceutical formulation, drug-delivery technology, natural products, and translational health research. I also develop open-source applications and tools for everyday problems, from AI-assisted workflows and productivity to accessibility and practical utilities.

I enjoy exploring the space between **experimental science, software engineering, and useful products**—turning ideas into research projects, reproducible workflows, or applications that people can actually use.

我叫**谷昱宁**，**字盈秋**，也可以直接叫我**盈秋**。目前我在法国**巴黎萨克雷大学（Université Paris-Saclay）药学院**攻读 **Development of Drugs and Health Products（D2HP）药物与健康产品开发硕士**。

我的学术兴趣涉及**药物制剂、药物递送技术、天然产物以及转化健康研究**。科研之外，我也喜欢开发开源软件：包括 AI 辅助开发工具、生产力应用、实时翻译工具，以及解决日常实际问题的小程序。

对我来说，药学研究与软件开发并非完全分离的两件事。我希望把**实验研究、数据分析、自动化和产品设计**结合起来，做出真正实用的东西。

## Open-source projects · 开源软件项目

My projects are driven by practical needs: **reduce repetitive work, make software easier to use, and give people more control over their tools and data**.

我的开源项目大多源于自己遇到的真实需求：**减少重复劳动、改善使用体验，并尽可能保留用户对工具与数据的控制权**。

### AI, learning and automation · AI、学习与自动化

**[DevRelay](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/DevRelay)** · Multi-model coding workflow orchestration / 多模型开发流程编排  
A local, auditable coordination layer for planning, implementation, review, and handoffs between coding agents. It aims to reduce manual copy-paste while keeping human oversight over the workflow.  
面向多模型软件开发的本地编排工具，衔接规划、实现与代码审阅流程，减少模型之间反复手动交接的工作，同时保留人工决策权。

**[LiveCaptions Translator — Classroom Edition / 课堂同传助手](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/LiveCaptions-Translator)** · Speech recognition, translation and study notes / 语音识别、翻译与课堂笔记  
A classroom-focused fork of [SakiRinn/LiveCaptions-Translator](https://github.com/SakiRinn/LiveCaptions-Translator), adapted for foreign-language lectures with real-time transcription, Chinese translation, topic summaries, slide-aware notes, and audio playback.  
基于原项目改造的**课堂场景专用分支**，支持外语课堂实时转录、中文翻译、分节总结、结合课件生成笔记及语音播报，并非原项目的官方版本。

### Apps and productivity · 应用与生产力

**[Evolune](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Evolune)** · Android & Wear OS  
A local-first mobile and wearable application, accompanied by an independently deployed [product website](https://evolune.yingqiu.me).  
面向 Android 与 Wear OS 的本地优先应用，配有独立部署的[产品官网](https://evolune.yingqiu.me)。

**[Pure Pomodoro / 纯粹番茄钟](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/pure-pomodoro)** · Windows desktop utility / Windows 桌面工具  
A lightweight, fully offline Pomodoro timer with a design inspired by the current Windows wallpaper, customizable sessions, and focus-friendly controls.  
轻量、完全离线的 Windows 番茄钟，支持从当前壁纸提取配色、自定义专注与休息时长，以及便捷的快捷键操作。

**[MP14Tools](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/MP14tools)** · Windows hardware and power management / 硬件与电源管理  
A compact utility built for the Xiaomi Book Pro 14 (2026), featuring touchpad and OEM-key remapping, display/power profiles, and background-process efficiency controls.  
为小米 Book Pro 14（2026）定制的系统工具，提供触摸板重按及 OEM 热键映射、显示与电源档位切换、后台进程效率模式等功能。

### Everyday utilities · 生活实用工具

**[Petit Coin · 方便点](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Petit-Coin)** · Flutter · iOS & Android  
A multilingual map-based application for locating public toilets in France, with filters for availability and accessibility, based on OpenStreetMap and municipal open data.  
基于 Flutter 的法国公共厕所地图应用，提供多语言界面以及开放状态、无障碍设施等筛选，使用 OpenStreetMap 与城市开放数据。

**[Holland2Stay Notifier](https://github.com/YingQiu0871/Holland2StayNotifier)** · Python · SQLite · Telegram  
A housing-availability notification tool that detects newly bookable Holland2Stay listings and delivers updates to subscribed Telegram groups.  
通过 Python、SQLite 与 Telegram 监测 Holland2Stay 新上架房源，并向订阅群组推送通知。
